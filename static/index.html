      How the pipeline decides

      A cascade: cheap, calibrated checks settle most claims; the expensive knowledge-graph and LLM stages run only when the evidence is ambiguous. On FEVER, 229 of 300 claims are settled by the NLI model without any arbitration call, and 93% of those verdicts are correct.
