      How the pipeline decides

      A cascade: cheap, calibrated checks settle most claims; the expensive knowledge-graph and LLM stages run only when the evidence is ambiguous. On FEVER, 78 of 100 claims are settled by the NLI model without any arbitration call, and 97% of those verdicts are correct.
